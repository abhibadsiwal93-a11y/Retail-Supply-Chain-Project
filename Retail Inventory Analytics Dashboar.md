**Retail Inventory Analytics Dashboard**

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes retail supply chain data to evaluate inventory performance, demand trends, seasonal patterns, and stock efficiency.

The objective was to identify overstock risk, measure inventory turnover, and understand monthly demand movement to support better inventory planning.

**Business Objective**

Retail businesses often face:

Excess stock holding

Poor demand visibility

Inefficient inventory rotation

This project aims to:

Compare demand vs current stock

Measure category-wise inventory turnover

Analyze monthly demand trends (2023–2024)

Detect seasonal patterns

Perform simple demand forecasting

**Tools Used**

Python → Demand trend analysis, seasonal pattern detection, simple forecasting

SQL → Data cleaning \& metric extraction

Power BI → KPI calculation, Inventory Turnover, Demand vs Stock analysis, interactive dashboard

**Key Analysis**

Monthly Demand Trend:

Identified demand movement across 2023–2024 and observed growth patterns.

Seasonal Pattern Analysis:

Detected recurring quarterly demand spikes.

Inventory Turnover:

Calculated stock rotation efficiency to highlight slow-moving categories.

Demand vs Stock:

Compared current inventory with actual demand to identify overstock risk.

Simple Forecasting:

Estimated short-term demand using historical trends.

**Dashboard Highlights**

**Business Recommendations**

Based on the analysis of inventory turnover, dead stock value, regional demand, and seasonal trends, the following actions are recommended:

Reduce Slow-Moving Inventory:

SKUs with very low turnover (0.03 ratio) indicate severe overstocking. Implement discounting, bundling, or controlled liquidation strategies to free blocked capital.

Prioritize High-Demand Products:

Fast-moving products (e.g., Product ID 4148) require higher reorder frequency and defined safety stock levels to avoid stockouts.

Optimize Regional Allocation:

Since the West region shows higher demand, inventory distribution should be demand-driven to prevent regional imbalance.

Align Procurement with Seasonality:

Use identified monthly and quarterly demand patterns to plan procurement cycles more accurately.

Improve Working Capital Efficiency:

Although dead stock is 3.8%, ₹2.14 crore tied in non-moving SKUs significantly impacts liquidity. Reducing idle inventory can improve cash flow and reinvestment capacity.

**Conclusion**

This analysis revealed clear inventory inefficiencies driven by slow-moving SKUs, regional demand imbalance, and capital tied in non-performing stock. While the dead stock percentage appears moderate (3.8%), the absolute financial impact is significant enough to affect liquidity and operational flexibility.

By aligning inventory planning with demand trends, seasonal patterns, and turnover performance, the business can improve stock rotation, reduce working capital blockage, and enable more data-driven supply chain decisions.
